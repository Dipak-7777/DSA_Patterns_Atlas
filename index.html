Revision checklistPrint this card for a wall sheet
Two Pointers

Sort first? Dedup? Which pointer moves? Write the decision, not the loop.

Sliding Window

Put valid() in one place. Shrink in while, not if. Track best on every step.

Binary Search

Define can(x). Your lo/hi invariant decides everything—stick to one template.

Graphs

Weighted? Negative? K stops? Choose BFS, Dijkstra, or Bellman-Ford deliberately.

Kadane / Prefix Sum

Contiguous = Kadane. Equals K or divisible = prefix map. Know when to swap.

Heap

K largest → min-heap of size K. K smallest → max-heap. Median stream → two heaps.

Backtracking

Choose → recurse → un-choose. Prune early and sort candidates to skip duplicates.

DP vs Greedy

Use DP when local greed fails; use greedy only when the safe local choice is proven.
